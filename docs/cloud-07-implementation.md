# Capacity, persistent quota and bounded job infrastructure

CLOUD.07 implements the Model 04 capacity producer over the existing Entitlement quota rows and Platform job rows. It adds no physical business table, SQL transport, public method or permissive policy source. Actual named plans and generated manifests remain the persistence boundary. The existing production routes, bootstrap Container allocation, historical class migrations, data and `workers.dev=false` are preserved.

## Ownership and composition

Entitlement owns `IQuotaKernelPort`, its persistent store and seven `entitlement.quota-kernel-*` plans. Current business authority authorizes the complete captured command and resolved period/policy; independent immutable object/segment/deletion authority verifies measured settlement. Actual `Used` and `Held` remain charged across restart, renewal, downgrade, cleanup scheduling and unknown results. All-or-none admissions accept at most eight dimensions. A stricter operator admission ceiling never increases the persisted customer limit and is bound by current authority. It is enforced in the same atomic reservation batch, rather than a separate headroom read.

Storage owns the production Platform adapter and five `platform.capacity-job-*` plans. Actual deployment composition supplies its signed executor, configured recovery generation, exact definition/input authority and current realm recovery-scan authority. `CapacityStorageBinding` is a trusted server composition boundary. It never resolves `FoundationOptions`, guesses a generation, fabricates a foreign module descriptor, or accepts the wake's realm as permission. Enabling `ARCFORGES_CAPACITY` without these actual producers fails startup. CLOUD.21 owns production executor/environment activation and current-generation composition; business quota policy/measurement sources remain their actual owning operations' responsibility.

Job create, claim and checkpoint use the real receipt, sequence and change-archive tail. Claims increment a monotonic fence, have a maximum 60-second lease and a bounded attempt count; exhausted jobs become durable dead letters. A slice is bounded by 100 items and a total 20 seconds, including setup, reconciliation and checkpoint. Each publishing owner additionally checks the actual current job holder/fence/lease, full owner/realm/workspace and original recovery generation inside its atomic batch. A replacement live same-owner lease can rebind a still-live quota reservation after the old lease expires; the old holder cannot publish. Scheduling state conveys no business authorization.

Accepted claim and checkpoint receipts are reconciled before retrying a wake. A lost claim acknowledgement resumes its original lease terms, and a lost checkpoint acknowledgement does not repeat the slice. Seven-day receipt retention is separate from lease expiry. Bounded 25/50 ms retries repeat only the exact receipt-safe immutable batch; cancellation after a durable write does not roll it back. Unknown outcomes retain their original identities.

The pacer preserves original command IDs on unavailable/unknown transport. Only a definite continuation creates fresh IDs. Recovery preserves a page on ambiguous/unavailable dispatch but advances past definitively denied/invalid/conflicting jobs; those rows are reevaluated when the bounded scan wraps. A refused head cannot indefinitely starve later authorized jobs. Worker internet access stays disabled and the existing closed provider/object/storage registries remain unchanged outside the admitted capacity host.

`CapacityWakeScheduler` owns its copied signing key and dedicated HTTP client. It admits at most two active and two queued requests, takes a total 15-second budget including the queue, and signs only after admission. Shutdown cancels queued requests without dispatch; a forwarded hint with a lost receipt remains unknown. Caller cancellation is checked after send and buffered EOF. Worker adapters copy bounded body buffers, check monotonic deadlines even across empty fragments, cancel losing reads/responses, and clear timers and abort listeners.

## Configuration and measurement

`CapacityProfileCodec` strictly validates exact `launch-capacity.v1` JSON, duplicate/unknown members, canonical uint64 representation, realm/source/workload hashes and verified provider ceilings. The selected proposal is four `standard-2` instances, 600-second sleep, 8-second readiness and 10-second first response. Per-instance limits are 64 output streams, 16 unary calls, 32 queued calls, eight jobs and two reserved control slots. These proposal constants do not alter the accepted bootstrap deployment or establish account authorization, achieved throughput or cost approval.

`CapacityAdmission` reads all seven real quota dimensions and physical D1 inventory. It computes exact integer pressure, preserves protected read/export/cancel/delete/settle/restore work, stops onboarding/capacity sales at 80% and growth at 90%, and records the 30-day headroom/180-day partition decision. A missing approved operator manifest or verified provider inventory refuses growth; it never substitutes zero accounting. Protected operator admission alone confers no business permission.

`CapacityMeasurementHarness` creates a real authorized persistent measurement job, schedules it through the signed adapter and reports exact historical workload/source/profile hashes with actual primary read count, mean, maximum and bounded histogram P95. A lost wake leaves the Ready row recoverable. Every report labels full launch acceptance `not-assessed`; SQLite and component observations are not paid D1/Container or whole-series L-16 proof.

`CapacityLoadHarness` implements the full launch workload configuration and a bounded open-loop scheduler. Its 64 unary workers and 128-item queue expose pressure rather than lowering the requested offered rate. It admits one run, authorizes the exact immutable run/config/profile/provider evidence through the actual operator owner, and refuses missing operation adapters before dispatch. Actual foreground/feed adapters return owned asynchronous connection handles; the harness bounds open/read deadlines, validates observations, closes every handle and awaits teardown before releasing run admission. Unexpected owner defects abort the run. The scheduler never retries unknown business mutations itself.

The append-only run artifact records exact realm, configuration, approved profile/workload/source hashes and every original operation ID before dispatch. Its 128 MiB bound prevents unbounded retention; a partial append fences the artifact against later ambiguous reuse. A cancelled/unknown command remains available for its actual owner's receipt reconciliation. Typed AOT reports distinguish offered, acknowledged, refused, unavailable, unknown, queue-rejected and invariant-failed observations, and report conservative 10 ms histogram P95 bounds. Unverified command acknowledgements, duplicate debits, acknowledged loss and skipped feed sequences fail measurement invariants.

The concrete `CapacityQuotaLoadOperation` measures actual authorized named-plan guarded reservations and primary reads through `IQuotaKernelPort`, then verifies exact immutable reservation terms from the real primary store. Replayed operation IDs do not create a second hold; changed terms are an invariant failure. Operator fixture policy binds an isolated retained budget/owner/period and immutable metadata workload. Fixture cleanup still needs real termination evidence. This infrastructure fixture is not a commercial debit or an invented implementation of absent session/feed/Sync/Simulator producers.

The whole launch workload remains 100 concurrent foreground sessions, 200 finite streams, 20 admitted commands/s and 50 primary reads/s for 60 minutes, a two-times burst for 60 seconds, ten simultaneous sync bootstraps and ten simulator runs. Command P95 must be at most two seconds and cold-start P95 at most ten seconds, with no acknowledged loss, skipped feed sequence or duplicate debit. Real metadata/R2 body sizes, fail/retry/cold-start, lag and reserved growth must be measured in the approved deployment. CLOUD.10/WP50 owns launch envelope approval and acceptance. CLOUD.07 ordinary component/production composition delivery remains separate from that acceptance.
